# Built-in sample Dataset

`ashen-crown.en.e2r.json` and `ashen-crown.ja.e2r.json` are byte-for-byte copies
of the canonical English and Japanese samples at
`e2r-spec/examples/ashen-crown.en.e2r.json` and
`e2r-spec/examples/ashen-crown.ja.e2r.json`, synchronized from E2R-SPEC commit
`25de41d2bd3b502d6e53b7ff775776d4a6e21ddf` on 2026-10-08. Their SHA-256
digests are respectively
`044187116106ce27178eb2457df8f0dad3ee68d6bb656800bede68900e306552` and
`745d3d293cc07cd84e076090d8f152deea69c5818f535f2cf9e9cbc51c65ec2f`.
E2R-SPEC remains the source of truth. The Home sample action selects the copy
matching the current ParameterPlotter UI locale; this does not translate or
rewrite Dataset-authored content and does not change general startup Handoff
locale precedence. See the [Home sample locale alignment record](../docs/home-sample-locale-alignment-ashen-crown-2026-10-09.md).
The bounded EN sample Human Browser Acceptance is recorded in the
[sample review closure](../docs/ashen-crown-bundled-sample-human-browser-closure-2026-10-08.md);
the original [sync result](../docs/ashen-crown-bundled-sample-sync-2026-10-08.md)
retains its earlier Pending status as historical evidence.

The E2R-SPEC [public sample provenance record](https://github.com/sukoyaka-dopeness/e2r-spec/blob/main/docs/public-samples/public-sample-provenance.md)
records the Ashen Crown sample's project authorship and redistribution
readiness, including the Human-adopted Dimension Assessments. ParameterPlotter
adds no sample facts or Dataset metadata.
